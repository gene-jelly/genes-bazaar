^If the district must close schools for a day or more due to hazardous weather, the school district will utilize weather day/s to provide for any
lost instructional days. Given the placement of weather days on May 29, June 1 and 2, there is a possibility that the school year could extend past
May 28th. Should there be no need to use the Weather Days on May 29, June 1 and 2, teacher post-planning days would be moved up to begin on
the first available non-instructional day during that week.
