## How obsessions work here

The bazaar is built for serial hyperfocus — weeks-long deep dives on a single topic, then a shift. Every obsession gets its own tag (like `calendar-fragmentation`) so you can see everything from one era of the work in one place. Shared facet tags (`research`, `policy`, `data-analysis`, `essay`, `dossier`) let you cut the same work a different way — "show me all the dossiers across all obsessions" or "show me every essay about schools."

Nothing here is permanent, and nothing here is disposable. The point is to tend each stall fully while it's the stall.
